# HST & AST Report

Every published chronicle's HST and ASTs with their own email addresses, to check the staff records and to reach the storytellers.

## Who can use this

Site admins, the Exec team and the Web team, on chronicles.owbn.net.

## How to get there

wp-admin on chronicles.owbn.net → **OWBN C&C** → **HST & AST Report**.

## The screen

- "N active chronicles. N have an HST or AST with no name or no email address."
- **Filter by chronicle, name or email** - narrows the table as you type.
- **Only chronicles with a missing name or email** - shows only those.
- **Export CSV** - the whole report as a spreadsheet file.
- One row per published chronicle, by name:
  - **Chronicle** - its name, which opens it, and its slug.
  - **HST (email)** - the HST's name and own email.
  - **Staff (emails)** - each AST's name and own email, or "None listed".
  - **Last Updated** - the day the chronicle's record last changed.
- "No name" and "No email" mark the gaps.
- Click a column heading to sort by it.

## Common tasks

### Find chronicles with a missing name or email

1. Tick **Only chronicles with a missing name or email**.
2. Click a chronicle's name and fill in the gap on its **Game, Schedule & Staff** tab.

### Get the list as a spreadsheet

1. Click **Export CSV**. The file, `owbn-hst-ast-<date>.csv`, has the same four columns, each person written as "Name <email>".

## Things to know

- The emails are the staff's own addresses, their **Actual Email**, not the public ones. Keep the report and the CSV among staff.
- Draft and decommissioned chronicles aren't in it.
- CMs aren't in it. OWBN Core's **Reports** → **Chronicle Staff** lists them.

## Troubleshooting

- **No HST & AST Report menu.** It's for site admins, the Exec team and the Web team, on the site that holds the chronicles.
- **Someone shows "No email" but the chronicle page shows one.** The page shows their **Display Email**; the report reads **Actual Email**. Fill it in on the chronicle.
