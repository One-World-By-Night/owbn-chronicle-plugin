# Pending Staff Changes

Staff changes waiting for approval: a new HST, CM, AST or sub-coordinator, or someone taken off, saved by anyone other than a site admin.

## Who can use this

Site admins, the Exec team and the Web team.

## How to get there

wp-admin → **OWBN C&C** → **Pending Changes**. A number next to it counts the changes waiting.

## The screen

- "N change set(s) awaiting approval."
- One card per chronicle or office with a change waiting:
  - Its name, which opens it, and whether it's a Chronicle or a Coordinator.
  - **Submitted by** - who saved the change, their email and when.
  - **Self-promotion detected** - they put themselves in a seat.
  - **Field** and **Pending Value** - each staff field the change touches, with everyone it would list.
  - **Approve** and **Reject**.
- "No pending staff changes. Nothing to review." when there are none.

The same change also shows as a bar on the chronicle's or office's own edit screen, with **Approve Changes** and **Reject Changes**.

## Common tasks

### Approve a change

1. Check the **Pending Value**: it's the full new list for that seat, not just the people added.
2. Click **Approve**. The staff change goes onto the record, and everyone it adds gets their role on sso.owbn.net.

### Reject a change

1. Click **Reject** and confirm.
2. The record keeps its staff as they were, and anyone the change would have removed gets their role back.

## Things to know

- Everything else in the same save went through at once; only the staff change waits.
- Anyone the change takes off loses their role as soon as it's saved, before anyone approves it.
- Each record holds one waiting change. A second save with another staff change replaces the first.
- A site admin's own staff changes never wait here. A site admin who saves new staff on a record with a change waiting replaces that change with their own.

## Troubleshooting

- **No Pending Changes menu.** Only site admins, the Exec team and the Web team see it.
- **Approve or Reject didn't take.** The page was open for a day or more. Reload it and click again.
