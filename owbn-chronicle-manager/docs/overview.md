# OWBN C&C Manager Overview

C&C Manager holds the record of every OWbN chronicle and coordinator office: who runs it, when and where it plays, its documents and its staff. Every other OWbN site reads those records, so a change saved here shows up everywhere.

## What it does

- **Chronicles** live on chronicles.owbn.net: each chronicle's description, genres, games, locations, documents and lists, and its HST, CM and ASTs.
- **Coordinator offices** live on council.owbn.net: each office's description, term, documents and lists, and its coordinator and sub-coordinators.
- **Staff roles.** Putting someone in a staff seat gives them the matching role on sso.owbn.net, and taking them out takes it away.
- **Approval.** A staff change made by anyone but a site admin waits in [Pending Staff Changes](help/pending-changes.md) until the Exec or Web team approves it.
- **Required documents.** Every chronicle lists its House Rules, Disciplinary Policy and Accessibility Policy, and the chronicle list shows which ones are missing.
- **Shared data.** Other sites read the records through OWBN Core: public names and emails only, never anyone's own email address.

## Who does what

- **HSTs, CMs and ASTs** keep their chronicle's record up to date, see [Editing a Chronicle](help/chronicle-edit.md).
- **Coordinators and sub-coordinators** keep their office's record up to date, see [Editing a Coordinator Office](help/coordinator-edit.md).
- **Site admins, the Exec team and the Web team** add chronicles and offices, approve staff changes, and set the fields only they can change: the slug, probationary, satellite, start date and region.
